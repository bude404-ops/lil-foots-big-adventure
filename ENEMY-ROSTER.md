# Lil Foots — Enemy Roster (BudE's picks, canon: Roster B, Sept 20 2026)

LAW (Enemy Doctrine, locked): all enemies are MADE-UP forest creatures, Mario-style. ZERO humans, skeptics, hunters, or gadget enemies — the v1 human era is retired forever. Enemies land at M3 (the "life" milestone): M1 clean floor → M2 course → M3 life.

## Region 1 — Pacific Northwest (Sasquatch turf)

**Boss: THE WENDIGO** — antlered, frost-breath, winter-hunger spirit of the old growth. Final course: 1-4 Old Growth Deep.

| Creature | Archetype | Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| **Barkling** | walker | Bark-covered walking stump, slow ground patrol, turns at ledges. The intro walker (solo first, combos later). |
| **Frostwing** | swooper | Perched ice-feathered owl; telegraphs, then dive-swoops along a fixed arc. |
| **Brambleboar** | charger | Bramble-mane boar; snorts (telegraph), then charges the player's lane. Always a gap/ledge to dodge into. |
| **Sporepuff** | turret | Stationary puff-shroom lobbing slow spore clouds. Defeatable by timing, not combat. |
| **Webwick** | ambusher | Web-dropping lurker hanging in vertical sections; triggers on approach, telegraphed by web shimmer. |

Rules baked into every enemy (Mario doctrine):
- Telegraphs first — no blind hits, no cheap deaths.
- Every enemy is bypassable; placement drives movement, not punishment.
- Enemies appear solo before they appear in combos.
- 1-1 introduces only ONE enemy type (walker), late in the course.

Current M2 course build keeps the hound as the placeholder walker (Barkling's slot) until the M3 art + AI pass replaces it with the real roster.
