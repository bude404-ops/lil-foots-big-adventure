# Boss Fight Design — SNOWCOIL, THE FROZEN KING (Region 2 finale: 2-4 The Summit Shiver)

> The moving-arena boss: the whole arena is his body's territory. Template: BOSS_WENDIGO.md.
> Bowser law: he loses, he grumbles, he stays a villain.

## The Arena — "The Hoard Glacier"

A hollow of packed blue ice at the summit, ringed by wind banners (arena gust timing is
readable off them, per the region's wind doctrine). At the back: his **hoard mound —
the yetis' stolen totems frozen in a spiral**. Semi-circular floor of ice sheets: the
region's low-friction feel IS the arena floor. Snowfall drifts sideways; the wind rises
each phase.

## Phase 1 — "The Ridge" (100% → 66%)

Snowcoil stays submerged. A moving ridge of powder crosses the floor — that's him,
under the snow, tracking warmth. The ridge telegraphs his eruption point ~1s ahead
(powder bulges). Erupt: he bursts up in a frozen geyser where the ridge ends.
Punish: each eruption leaves his head exposed on the ice ~1.5s before he dives —
the hit window. Three windows to phase 2.

## Phase 2 — "The King Surfaces" (66% → 33%)

He surfaces and coils along the arena rim — the hoard mound totems glowing along his
body (trophy rings, per his design sheet). Attacks alternate:
- **Blizzard breath** — a sweeping cone that rides the wind banners' gust direction.
- **Coil slam** — his tail whips the floor in a ring; telegraph: his icicle crown glints.
Punish: he periodically plants his head on the ice to sneer ("MINE.") — a smug window.
Three more hits to phase 3.

## Phase 3 — "The Broken Hoard" (33% → 0%)

Sore-king tantrum: he whips his own hoard mound — frozen totems break loose and
scatter across the arena as temporary platforms (ice friction applies). He goes fast
now: two ridges at once, then full-coil lunges across the arena width. The kill loop:
lure a lunge through a standing totem — he clips his own trophy, staggers, window.
Twice more and the king falls.

## Defeat

He slithers into the deep glacier, snarling, tail dragging, hoard empty — the totems
thaw upright where they landed, the central totem RELIGHTS, the blizzard breaks into
gold evening light, and the flag gate + portal to Region 3 open. Grudge held.
