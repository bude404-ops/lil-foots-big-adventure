# Items & Abilities (Draft v1)

> The collectibles and pickups doctrine. Everything grows naturally from the world —
> no gadgets, no tech. All names TBD by BudE.

## Collectibles

- **Big Token** *(locked)* — the trail's shining reward: a cedar coin bearing the
  glowing footprint. The course's main collectible and the score of a good run.
- **Footprint Marks** (draft) — small glowing paw-press tokens scattered along the
  trail, the "coins" of the world. Painted as pressed glows in moss and snow.
- **Trailberries** (draft) — bright red-gold berries growing on bushes along the
  course. Each one gives an extra heart (carry up to 3). Bounce a berry bush and it
  shakes a couple loose — a small secret everywhere.
- **Star Dew** (draft) — a rare hanging dewdrop that lights up like a tiny star.
  Drinking it gives a short sparkle-run where nothing can touch you. One per course,
  always hidden past an optional route.

## Hearts & getting hit

- Lil Foots start with 2 hearts (draft); Trailberries add up to 3.
- Getting hit costs a heart with the squash-pop (established paper animation) and a
  brief flash of invincibility. Zero hearts = the Lil Foot tumbles back to the
  course's checkpoint totem — the established checkpoint system, unchanged.

## Abilities (the shared moveset — locked doctrine)

All three Lil Foots share the same tuned core: **run, jump, climb** — one feel, no
character stat differences (per the current game law). Per-region movement feels
(Region 1 streams, Region 2 ice + wind, etc.) live in each region's concept doc.

## The rule for new items

If it doesn't grow on the trail, drip from a tree, or glow in a totem's light, it
doesn't belong in the world. One look at a pickup and a kid knows what it does.
