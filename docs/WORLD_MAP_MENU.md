# World Map & Menu Direction (Draft v1)

> The menus ARE the world — no abstract screens. All TBD by BudE.

## The Hidden Hollow = the main menu

The family den IS the menu backdrop: warm cedar, campfire light, the World Trail
totem at its heart. The player picks a character by literally picking who walks to
the totem (the three Lil Foots are doing den chores around the fire — Lily map-reading,
Buddy snacking, Emma upside-down in the rafters). Selected character gets the idle
animation treatment (locked menu law: animated idles, not static portraits).

## The World Trail totem = the map

The level select is the carved totem itself: five carved regions on the pole, each
course a small painted trail marker on its band. Cleared courses glow; the boss
course carries its villain's mark until beaten. Camera dollies up the pole as regions
unlock — the map reading like a journey up a trail (the naming doctrine, made literal).

- Region bands unlock in order; within a band, courses unlock sequentially.
- A cold (unlit) band shows a hint of its villain's silhouette in the carved grain —
  the kid sees the boss coming before the region opens.

## Menu stings & flow

- The Hollow theme plays at the totem; touching a region band shifts it to that
  region's variation (the one-family melody at work).
- Pause menu = the character sitting by the trail, sketching the map; resume is a
  hand-off back onto the path.
- Loading between regions = a short painted transition of the family walking the
  trail (with the two studio splash cards at cold boot: Bigfoot Entertainment + BudE
  Vision, locked).

## Post-game map

All five bands lit; the map opens for free trail-hunting (100% routes, secret
groves, rematch taunts left on the trail by the villains). The World Trail totem's
five lights become the save-game's badge of honor.
