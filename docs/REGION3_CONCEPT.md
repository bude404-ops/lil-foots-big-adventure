# Region 3 Concept — "THE RED DUST" (Outback / Yowie homeland)

> The desert region: red-ochre scrub and canyon gorges, dusk-gold light, and the game's
> show-off villain. Template: REGION2_CONCEPT.md. All names/drafts TBD by BudE.

## The Look

Region 2 was blue-white silence; Region 3 is warm noise: **red-ochre earth, sun-bleached
scrub, canyon banding, and a golden dust haze that softens every edge.** Ember-reds and
dusk-golds, deep violet shadows in the gorges, and at dusk the whole palette tilts to
rose. Parallax bands: far mesas, mid gorge walls, near scrub plain, foreground spinifex
clumps. Weather character: the wind here is playful — it kicks dust devils and drives
the tumbleweed hazards.

## The Locals — the Yowies

Small, shaggy, quick-grinning hill folk whose trails went cold when Sunsprint started
winning everything. Draft trail-keeper: **Cousin Roo**, a wiry yowie trail-runner who
gives tips at sprint-speed ("Shimmer tells ya where 'e's goin' — read the waves, mate.").

## The Courses (trail-map naming, drafts)

- **3-1 Scrubline Start** — the safe opening: shimmer-reading taught on gentle flats.
- **3-2 Gorge Crossing** — canyon walls and gap hops; heat shimmer bends the sightlines.
- **3-3 Emu Run** — momentum course: chase-runs with tumbleweed bounce chains.
- **3-4 Dusk Basin** — the finale: Sunsprint's tag ring at red dusk.

## New Movement Feels

1. **Shimmer-sight** — heat distortion marks hidden gaps and hot zones: the region's
   "read the world" skill (used again by Sunsprint's shadow-vs-shimmer tells).
2. **Tumbleweed** — rolling hazards that hurt if they bowl you, but a timed touch
   bounces you skyward — obstacle AND trampoline, the region's signature toy.
3. **Thermal updrafts** — sun-baked columns that lift; visible as rising ember motes.
   Region 3's version of climbing.
