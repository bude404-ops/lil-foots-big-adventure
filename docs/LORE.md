## 1. The Premise

A hand-painted, storybook platformer set in a world of Bigfoot legends. Three young
Sasquatch siblings — the Lil Foots — leave the safety of their hidden valley to restore
the trails of the world, one legend-region at a time. Where humans see mist, they see
footprints. Every region is a real cryptid homeland, treated as the true history that
"science never wrote down."

**Tone:** cozy but adventurous — PNW-misty mornings, golden-hour light, a warm trail
through a wild world. Wonder over menace. Danger exists, but the forest is home first.

**Style law:** stylized painterly, one-brush world — single palette per region, shared
light sources, atmospheric perspective, organic non-tiling silhouettes. Characters and
world read as living paper cutouts in a 2.5D diorama (Paper-Mario technique, all art 100% original).

## 4. The Creatures (Enemy Doctrine)

All enemies are **made-up forest creatures** native to their region's biome — in the
spirit of classic platformer enemy design. No humans, no skeptics, no gadgets, no tech.

Archetypes (Mario-lineage structure, original bodies):
- **Walker** — patrols the trail.
- **Swooper** — arcs out of trees, off ledges.
- **Charger** — winds up, then rushes.
- **Turret** — rooted in place, lobs projectiles.

## 5. World Rules (the Laws)

1. **Enemies arrive at the "life pass"** — a course ships as geometry first, then gets its creatures.
2. **Anything that MOVES or CHANGES STATE is a sprite; anything that just stands in the world is painted into the world** — gates, portals, totems, giants, rock spines.
3. **Floats anchor to parents** — floating platforms read as the lit front edge of a real structure (rock face, branch) with the parent painted a layer behind.
4. **Course flow is Mario-inspired technique, all assets 100% original** — safe open, teach the move, escalate, secrets, finale at flag + portal.
5. **Maps are layered dioramas** — static vista wash, parallax depth bands, gameplay plane, foreground cutouts.
6. **Character art is BudE's original art** — the in-game cutouts are his approved pieces, always.

the reason its trail went cold. Light all five regions and the world's paths open again.

## 7. Glossary

- **Big Token** — the trail's shining reward: a cedar coin bearing the glowing footprint.
- **Checkpoint totem** — a mossy cedar marker that holds your place on the trail.
- **Flag gate** — course finish: cedar pole, deep-green flag, gold emblem.
- **Portal** — the mossy megalith archway between courses and regions.
- **Old Growth Deep** — the ancient heart-wood of Region 1; the Wendigo's ground.
