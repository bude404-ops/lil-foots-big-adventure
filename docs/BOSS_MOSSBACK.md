# Boss Fight Design — MOSSBACK, THE STILL KING (Region 4 finale: 4-4 The Still Deep)

> The wake-up dance. A boss who barely moves — until the fight earns his attention.
> Bowser law: he loses, grumbles that he was only resting, stays a villain.

## The Arena — "The Throne Pool"

The heart of the still blackwater: a ring of lily pads stepping around his sunken
throne — the stolen totems stacked beneath the surface as his seat, their faint glows
underwater like lanterns. Pads sink slowly when stood on (the region's rhythm-movement
feel is the arena). Firefly light. He does not get up. Not yet.

## Phase 1 — "The Ripples" (100% → 66%)

Mossback doesn't move a muscle. His attacks are ripples:
- **Tail-flick waves** — ring waves cross the pads; hop the wave crest.
- **Breath bubbles** — bubbles rise and pop into splash columns; the pop spot bubbles
  ~0.8s first (telegraphed, fair).
The player's job: stomp the drifting **snore bubbles** that escape his beard —
popping them wakes him cranky. Each stomp: his eyes open one notch. Three stomps.

## Phase 2 — "The Sinking Court" (66% → 33%)

He submerges fully; the pool level rises and the pad ring drifts apart — footwork
tightens (sink-timing matters now). He surfaces where the snore bubbles clustered,
moss beard trailing — the surface is a grumble-stagger window: 3 hits before he sinks
again with an insult ("Rude."). Loop twice more to phase 3.

## Phase 3 — "The Cranky Court" (33% → 0%)

Fully awake and OUT of patience — the fight becomes a proper bout: slow colossal steps
that tilt the pad ring (drift navigation), plus **beard grabs**: the moss beard snaps
short-range at a lunge distance (the vine-whip of this region). His posture IS the
tell: he leans a beat before a step or a snap — readable weight, the boss design doc's
"menace through posture" law, live. He's slow everywhere except the grab.

## Defeat

He sinks back onto his throne of totems, muttering that in HIS day, the swamp stayed
quiet, and it's the younger generation that's the problem — a villain who refuses to
admit he lost. The stolen totems float free and right themselves, the central one
RELIGHTS, the still water gets its current back, flag gate + portal to Region 5.
