# Villain Design Sheets (Draft v1)

> Visual design descriptions for the five big bads — written to feed the future art
> passes. Each follows the painterly style law and its region's palette. All designs
> TBD by BudE.

## 1. THE WENDIGO, Warden of the Long Winter (Region 1)

Tall and gaunt, antlered silhouette breaking the treeline. Bark-textured hide
frosted with ice, long arms ending in root-claw hands, a faint ice-blue glow behind
hollow eyes. Painted in Region 1's mossy-green palette but desaturated and rimmed in
frost white. Moves with terrible patience — it is never in a hurry, and that is the
scary part.

## 2. SNOWCOIL, the Frozen King (Region 2)

A colossal glacier serpent of packed-ice segments, teal-white with deep slate
shadow. A snowy mane ruff behind the head, a crown of icicle horns, and the yetis'
stolen totems frozen along his coils like trophy rings — the hoard he wears. Moves
slow and smug on the surface, blindingly fast beneath the snow.

## 3. SUNSPRINT, the Tag Tyrant (Region 3)

A lean, long-legged ember sprite — roadrunner-jackrabbit energy — wrapped in
heat-shimmer distortion. Dust-devil mane, gilded sun emblem burnt bright on his
chest, permanent smug grin. Red-ochre and gold on the Region 3 desert palette.
Never stops moving: pacing, preening, taunting mid-fight.

## 4. MOSSBACK, the Still King (Region 4)

A colossal moss-shelled swamp elder — part ancient turtle, part old gator, all
disapproval. Lily-pad patches and hanging moss beard, half-lidded droopy eyes that
barely open, breath that lifts the water in slow ripples. Deep green-black palette
with warm firefly accents. He barely moves — and the whole swamp goes still with him.

## 5. VINEHEART, the Great Collector (Region 5, finale)

A mountainous strangler-fig titan, the oldest living thing in the world's oldest
jungle. Deep green body, violet bloom clusters along his vines, and at his chest a
slow-blinking heart-shaped hollow that glows warm — the loneliest light in the game.
Wrapped in the totems and trail markers of a thousand years of "collecting." Speaks
slowly, moves rarely, and takes everything personally.

## Shared design laws

- All five are painted in their region's own palette law — villains never break the
  color script, they ARE its shadow side.
- Menace reads through posture and scale (the slow blink, the unhurried step), never
  gore or darkness.
- Each one visually carries the stolen totems somewhere on its body — the player
  sees exactly what they're fighting to take back.
