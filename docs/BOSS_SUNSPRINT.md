# Boss Fight Design — SUNSPRINT, THE TAG TYRANT (Region 3 finale: 3-4 Dusk Basin)

> The fight IS a game of tag, and he cheats. He's "it," he taunts, and he never
> accepts a loss. Bowser law: he loses, he storms off still claiming he let you win.

## The Arena — "The Basin Ring"

A sun-baked rock basin at red dusk, ringed with painted tag banners. The floor is
cracked clay (grips, unlike Region 2) dotted with natural hop-stones and three
tumbleweed mounds (regional hazards — they tumble with the wind and bounce you
skyward if timed right). His trophy pouch — the stolen totems — hangs at his hip.

## Phase 1 — "Tag" (100% → 66%)

He dashes shimmer-lines across the ring (heat-shimmer telegraph traces his path
~0.8s early — the region taught reading shimmer in 3-1). He's IT: a tag = a hit and
he takes a lap of showboating. You score by tagging HIM: the loop is to hold near a
tumbleweed, let his dash line lock on, then bounce over the dash and tag his back as
he passes under. Three tags to phase 2.

## Phase 2 — "The Cheat" (66% → 33%)

He cheats: pulls the stolen totems from his pouch and uses them — as decoy shimmer
copies (two fake Sunsprints dash the ring; the REAL one casts a shadow, the fakes
don't — the tell), and as slam weights he overhand-throws. He still has to run past
you between dashes: same tag loop, now reading shadow instead of shimmer. Three tags.

## Phase 3 — "Sore Loser Sprint" (33% → 0%)

Full tantrum: he sprints the rim non-stop in a heat vortex, too fast to tag — so you
use the basin: tumbleweed mounds, placed on the ring's notches, become bounce cuts.
Bounce over the ring's edge stones to cut the circle and tag him crossing the gaps.
Three rim tags and he goes down.

## Defeat

He huffs, stamps, throws the stolen totems on the ground ("FINE. TAKE them. You only
won because I WANTED you to."), and storms off into the dusk at top speed, still
showboating. The totems right themselves, the central one RELIGHTS, dusk cools into
evening, flag gate + portal to Region 4. He will absolutely demand a rematch someday.
