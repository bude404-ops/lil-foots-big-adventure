# Region 2 Concept — "THE FROSTCAP PEAKS" (Himalaya / Yeti homeland)

> The mountain region: snowfield-glacier vocabulary, alpine palette, two new movement
> feels (ice friction + wind), and the game's second big bad. All names and designs
> are drafts for BudE's call.

## The Look

Region 1 was moss-green and gold in mist. Region 2 flips the value script: **an alpine
world of blue-whites, glacier teal, and slate shadow, with warm gold reserved for the
trail's light** — totems, banners, the sun's last hour on the snow. Parallax bands
(Region 1 law, kept): far white peaks, mid icefalls, near snowfield plane, foreground
icicle-ledges. Weather is a character: snowfall drifts, wind streaks the powder, and on
windy courses banners pull in the gust direction.

Palette anchors (draft): glacier teal #A8C8D8 zone, powder white floors, deep slate
blue shadows, ember-gold trail light.

## The Locals — the Yeti

First region where the cryptid isn't only the player's kin: **yetis are the locals**
whose trails went cold. Friendly, shaggy, mountain-quiet. Draft trail-keeper:
**SHERU**, an old yeti who lights trail banners and speaks in short weather-wisdom
("Wind says go. Mountain says wait."). NPC cutout-sprites per the movement law
(they move, so they're sprites).

## The Courses (trail-map naming, drafts)

- **2-1 Snowline Steps** — the safe opening: teaching the new ice feel on gentle steps.
- **2-2 Windcarve Shelf** — wind mechanics introduced: gusts push mid-jump, telegraphed by banner direction and powder flowing sideways.
- **2-3 Icefall Crossing** — climbing tech peaks: ice-wall climbs over open chasms, frost swoopers between shelves.
- **2-4 The Summit Shiver** — the finale: a storm-veiled glacier descent into SNOWCOIL's cozy hollow.

## New Movement Feels (the region's identity)

1. **Ice friction** — snowfields grip, ice sheets don't. Introduced in 2-1, mastered by 2-4. (Prototyped in the Wendigo phase-2 floor slicks — the boss teaches the region before you arrive.)
2. **Wind gusts** — periodic lateral force during gusts; banners + snow grain tell you direction and timing. A course can be "read" like a weather report.
3. **Ice-wall climbing** — climbable frozen faces (grips = frost patches) where a wrong handhold drops into powder, not death. Region 2's version of Region 1's float-anchor climbing.

## Creature Roster (drafts, TBD by BudE — walker/swooper/charger/turret archetypes)

- **ICEKNUCKLE** (walker) — a squat frozen boulder-beast that patrols the snowline.
- **POWDERWING** (swooper) — a wind-borne raptor that dives out of snow-veils.
- **GALEHARE** (charger) — a long-eared snow hare that rockets across slicks.
- **RIMECAP** (turret) — a frost-fungus rooted to ice walls that pops icicle shots.

## Big Bad — SNOWCOIL, THE SLEEPING STORM

Full sheet lives in `docs/VILLAINS.md`. In concept: a colossal glacier serpent who
got woken mid-nap and is VERY grumpy about it — every sneeze is a blizzard. The fight
ends not with a defeat but with Snowcoil yawning, curling up warm, and snoozing
happily; the blizzard clears into gold evening light and Snowcoil becomes a big
snoring friend of the region. Arena concept: the fight moves —
Snowcoil dives between snow shelves and re-surfaces around the arena, so the player
reads the avalanche telegraphs (falling powder columns) and fights on shifting sheets
of the same slick-ice feel the region taught. Ends, like every region, with the cold
totem relit and the blizzard breaking into gold evening light.

## Region 2 vs Region 1 (the escalation logic)

| | Region 1 | Region 2 |
|---|----------|----------|
| Feel | discovery | mastery under pressure |
| Floor | moss + streams grip | ice slides, wind pushes |
| Secrets | hidden groves | storm-covered ledges |
| Boss | shivery stalker (dodge the line) | sleepy sneezer (dodge the sneeze) |
| Light | morning mist gold | last-hour alpengold |
