# Region 4 Concept — "THE STILL WATER" (Everglades / Skunk Ape homeland)

> The swamp region: the quiet one. Rhythm-movement over blackwater, firefly light,
> and a boss who barely opens his eyes. Template: REGION2_CONCEPT.md. All TBD by BudE.

## The Look

**Deep green-black water, mangrove root cathedrals, sawgrass flats, and firefly
accents like warm sparks in the mist.** The palette runs darkest of the five regions
— moss-dark greens, tea-black water, grey-green mist — with gold firefly light and
the totems' glows carrying all the warmth. Parallax bands: far mangrove skyline,
mid mist bands, near pad-hop water, foreground roots and reeds.

## The Locals — the Skunk Apes

Shy, gentle, big-footed marsh folk (distant cousins of the family, the lore wink).
Draft trail-keeper: **Cousin Tulley**, a slow-talking skunk ape who poles a raft and
gives directions like riddles ("The pad that sinks fastest holds the most light.").

## The Courses (trail-map naming, drafts)

- **4-1 Sawgrass Path** — the safe opening: pad-hop rhythm taught slow.
- **4-2 Mangrove Tangle** — root mazes and light gaps; firefly swarms mark safe lines.
- **4-3 Firefly Hollow** — dark-water crossing lit by fireflies; longest rhythm course.
- **4-4 The Still Deep** — the finale: Mossback's throne pool.

## New Movement Feels

1. **Pad rhythm** — lily pads sink slowly underfoot: keep moving, learn the beat.
   The region's core feel (and Mossback's arena).
2. **Raft drift** — drifting log sections on slow current: steering without speed.
3. **Firefly sight** — in dark sections, fireflies trace the safe route; the region's
   "read the world" skill, and the softest secret-hunting in the game.

## Creature Roster (drafts, TBD by BudE)

- **MUDSLOGGER** (walker) — a sludgy marsh-stomper that wades the flats.
- **LEAFGLIDER** (swooper) — a leaf-camouflaged glider off the mangrove boughs.
- **SNAPPERBOUGH** (charger) — a mangrove limb that lunges when you cross its shadow.
- **SPITTOAD** (turret) — a marsh toad that pops spit-seeds from the reeds.
