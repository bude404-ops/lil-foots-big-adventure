# Region 5 Concept — "THE DEEP GREEN" (Amazon / Mapinguari homeland)

> The finale region: the oldest jungle in the world, where every trail the game taught
  converges. Template: REGION2_CONCEPT.md. All names/drafts TBD by BudE.

## The Look

**The deepest, richest palette of the game: layered jungle greens, violet bloom
clusters, teal river light, and god rays through the canopy.** Where Region 4 was
dark-quiet, Region 5 is dark-LUSH — every surface alive. Parallax bands: far canopy
mists, mid buttress-root walls, near river terraces, foreground hanging vines. The
region's light signature: the totems and the villain's heart hollow are the only
two warm glows in a green world.

## The Locals — the Mapinguari

Tall, ancient, slow-moving jungle guardians (the oldest of the cryptid folk).
Draft trail-keeper: **Old Balsa**, a river-elder mapinguari who knows Vineheart's
whole history and speaks like the trees grow ("He was not always a collector.
But he was always... alone.").

## The Courses (trail-map naming, drafts)

- **5-1 Rivermouth Steps** — the safe opening: raft currents and giant-flower bounces.
- **5-2 Canopy Road** — vine-loop swings and bloom-cluster lines; the region's momentum course.
- **5-3 Bloom Labyrinth** — a jungle maze told in bloom language (violet blooms now mean
  the danger/trail signs the finale will use against Vineheart).
- **5-4 The Hollow at the Heart** — the finale: the game's last climb to the world-tree.

## New Movement Feels

1. **Vine-loop swings** — grab and release momentum swings, the finale's traversal verb.
2. **River current** — flowing water sections: steer the raft, ride the drift.
3. **Giant-flower bounce** — jungle trampolines; timing-based, the region's tumbleweed.

## Creature Roster (drafts, TBD by BudE)

- **GLOAMSTALKER** (walker) — a shadow-striped prowler of the undergrowth.
- **BLOOMHOPPER** (swooper) — a flower-backed hopper that leaps between blooms.
- **THICKETTUMBLE** (charger) — a balled thicket-beast that rolls the river terraces.
- **SEEDSPIT** (turret) — a cannon-pod plant that spits slick seeds.

## Big Bad — VINEHEART, THE GREAT COLLECTOR

Full sheet: VILLAINS.md + BOSS_VINEHEART.md. The finale region's logic: every skill
of the five regions returns in his fight — the boss IS the game's graduation exam.

## Finale note

Region 5 ends the story arc: five totems relit, the World Trail totem at home lights
all five regions, and the game loops into post-game trail-hunting with all five
regions open. Five villains, five grudges, and a sequel hook for each of them.
