## The Hidden Hollow (home, draft name)

The family's warm cedar valley, tucked behind a waterfall veil where the moss grows
gold. Cedar den, berry terraces, a wood-smoke fire that never goes out, and the
**World Trail totem** at its heart — a tall pole carved with five regions, whose
lights come on as each region is saved. This is the game's home/hub: the family den,
the gate to Region 1, and the spot where saved regions appear on the trail map.

## The Three

All three share the same moveset (run, jump, climb — one tuned feel, locked). The
differences are personality and story presence, not stats.

### LILY — the eldest
Steady, sure-footed, the trail leader. She reads a forest like a map — which is why
the family always knows where the safe crossings are. Her verdict is the one the
other two wait for. In-game feel: nothing rattles her — she's the "default" pick, the
one the tutorial voice lives in.

### BUDDY — the heart
Curious, brave, first to leap. Buddy never met a ledge he didn't want to check under
for snacks. The one who finds the fun in a bad situation and pulls the other two
into it, sometimes literally by the hand. In-game feel: the energy of the group.

### EMMA — the spark
Quick, playful, finds every secret path. Emma sees the gap in the brambles and the
wrong-turned root before anyone else. Fastest to laugh, first to say the thing the
grown-ups won't. In-game feel: the explorer's pick — flavor-wise, she'd be the one
who "senses" secrets.

## Story voice

The family never fights villains out of anger — they do it because the trails are
theirs to take care of. Courage in this world looks like walking into the cold woods
with your siblings beside you.
