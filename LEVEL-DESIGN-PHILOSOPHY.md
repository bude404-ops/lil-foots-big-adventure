# Lil Foots: Big Adventure — Level Design Philosophy

Bude's locked doctrine for all level design (Sept 17, 2026). An ORIGINAL Lil Foots experience — no Mario, Nintendo assets, characters, levels, layouts, sounds, branding, or copyrighted designs.

## Core Level Length
- Standard level: 2–4 minutes for a first-time player.
- Advanced level: 4–6 minutes.
- Boss level: 3–6 minutes.
- Bonus/secret level: 30 seconds–2 minutes.
- Never stretch levels to make them longer. Prioritize density, variety, and fun.

## Core Level Formula
**TEACH → PLAY → DEVELOP → CHALLENGE → FINALE → REWARD**

1. **TEACH** — Introduce the level's primary mechanic safely. No unnecessary punishment. Visual cues, simple examples.
2. **PLAY** — Use the mechanic naturally. Basic enemies, obstacles, collectibles, platforming. Build confidence.
3. **DEVELOP** — Combine the primary mechanic with previously learned mechanics. Gradual complexity. Alternate routes, optional secrets.
4. **CHALLENGE** — Strongest combination of mechanics. Tests timing, movement, enemy awareness, platforming. Difficulty from understanding and execution, not cheap traps.
5. **FINALE** — Memorable gameplay sequence: large platforming challenge, chase, enemy encounter, environmental event, or unique set piece. Must feel different from the opening.
6. **REWARD** — Satisfying ending. Collectibles, progression, animation, sound, effects, story. Reaching the goal must feel meaningful.

## Level Density (standard level)
- 3–5 major gameplay sections.
- 1 primary mechanic; 1–3 supporting mechanics.
- Multiple enemy encounters; collectibles.
- At least 1 secret or alternate route.
- At least 1 memorable gameplay moment.
- Clear beginning and ending.
- Pacing: EASY → EASY → MODERATE → MODERATE → HARD → FINALE (never equal difficulty throughout).

## Checkpoints
- ~Every 1–2 minutes.
- Never make players repeat a large section after dying.
- Checkpoints = meaningful progress. Restarting after death is FAST.

## Platforming
- Readable jumps; show landing areas before the player commits. No blind jumps on normal routes.
- Different jump distances and heights. Stationary + moving platforms. Occasional vertical sections.
- Environmental objects as gameplay elements.
- Reward skilled movement; never require it for basic completion.

## Enemy Design
- Introduce each enemy alone before combining threats.
- Recognizable behaviors; placement creates movement decisions.
- Don't fill every screen. Mix combat and platforming. Skilled players can bypass some.

## Collectibles
- Easy (main route), exploration (optional areas), difficult (advanced movement), hidden (observation).
- Never random placement.

## Secrets
Every standard level has at least one meaningful secret: hidden rooms, alternate paths, breakable walls, hidden platforms, underground areas, vertical bonus areas, risk/reward routes, short challenge rooms. Secrets reward curiosity.

## Player Flow
Alternate constantly: running → jumping → platforming → enemy encounters → exploration → collecting → safe breathers → bigger challenges. Never the same action for an extended period.

## Visual Communication
Gameplay objects must be visually distinguishable from decoration via: silhouettes, lighting/value contrast, environmental framing, directional composition, animation, particles, signage, character reactions. Background must never compete with gameplay.

## World Building
Every world has a distinct environment + gameplay identity: Forest, Mountain, Swamp, Mine, River, Ancient ruins, Snow, Volcanic, Bigfoot territory, Industrial. Each world introduces mechanics appropriate to its environment.

## Mobile-First Design
Lil Foots works extremely well on phones: large readable objects, responsive controls, no excessive precision requirements, minimal visual clutter, understandable deaths, extremely fast restarts, levels completable in a short session.

## Replayability
Never length. Instead: collectible completion, secret discovery, speed challenges, no-damage challenges, alternate routes, bonus objectives, completion ratings based on objective achievement (NOT an overall quality score). A normal player finishes quickly; a completionist has reasons to replay.

## Level Feel
The player constantly experiences: **DISCOVER → LEARN → TRY → SUCCEED → EXPLORE → MASTER → REWARD**. The player is becoming better at playing Lil Foots, not just fighting more enemies.

## Most Important Rule
A short, polished, highly replayable 3-minute level is better than a boring 8-minute level. Never add length to meet a target. Every section must earn its place. Every level must answer: **"What is fun about this level that the player will remember?"**
