# Lil Foots: Big Adventure — Design Doc

**Status:** Design phase COMPLETE (locked by Bude, Sept 16-17 2026). Next: vertical slice build.
**Owner:** Bude (@BudE404) — all content decisions are Bude's. If it's not from Bude, it's not in this game.
**Repo:** bude404-ops/lil-foots-big-adventure (private). Built for the BIG Network.

## Style
- 2.5D side-scrolling platformer (Mario-style): gameplay locked to a 2D run/jump plane, rendered with 3D-model look and thick-outline cartoon art.
- World: misty Pacific-Northwest-style Bigfoot country — giant cedars, ferns, mossy boulders, teal fog.
- Lore basis: BIGFOOT lore (small sasquatches, cryptid-hunters, hoax culture). Global cryptid lore (yeti/yowie/skunk ape/mapinguari) = future region worlds.

## REGION STRUCTURE (Bude, Sept 18 2026: "we are making these maps region specific based on where bigfoots are around the world")
Maps are REGION-SPECIFIC — each region is themed on real-world Bigfoot/cryptid geography. Every region gets its own art skin (sky, treeline, terrain palette, weather/atmosphere) built in the cast's thick-outline cartoon style. Region art stays canon once Bude approves it.
- **REGION 1: PACIFIC NORTHWEST, US** (Bude: "the first region needs to be the PNW") — Sasquatch home turf. Overcast ink-outlined sky, distant Cascade ridges with a snow-capped volcano, dense outlined Douglas-fir wall, drifting mist banks, mossy greens + teal fog. v0.18 skin shipped (overcast sky plate, fir strip, code ridges/volcano/mist).

## Footprints (SIMPLIFIED by Bude, Sept 17 ~1:05 AM: "I ment Bigfoot prints as logos or spots they dont need to be a in game mechanic")
- Footprints = DECORATION / BRANDING ONLY — logos, decals, background spots, menu motifs. NOT a gameplay mechanic.
- THE BIG ONE — classic giant cryptid print (long, narrow, 5 toes, deep mud): the print-LOGO motif. The Big One is never seen — only his prints.
- LIL FOOT PRINTS — tiny cute versions as background spots.
- CARL'S FAKES — identical plaster decoys, one toe wrong + crossed out: background comedy.
- THE TRAIL — walking-gait trails into the mist as level-opening set dressing.

## Core Loop + Level End (LOCKED by Bude, Sept 17 ~1:05 AM)
- **Main mechanic = pure Mario**: run and jump through the map to reach the end. No gimmicks, no stealth systems.
- **LEVEL END = THE FLAGPOLE**: flagpole at the top of a hill — grab it to finish the level (classic Mario flagpole moment).
- **FLAG = BIG NETWORK WORDMARK** (Bude, Sept 17 ~1:15 AM: "i dont want the dog paws in it we are using the bignetwork logo") — dark flag, bold glowing BIG letters (Space Grotesk, cyan-green). NO paw prints on the flag.
- **PORTAL TO NEXT LEVEL** — glowing portal in a stone arch at the hill's base; the Lil Foot enters it to reach the next level.
