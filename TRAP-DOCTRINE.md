# Lil Foots: Big Adventure — Environmental Trap Doctrine (APPROVED by Bude, Sept 18 2026)

Every region gets its own trap set. Six universal archetypes, reskinned per region so each region
feels new but plays fair. Traps obey the Level Design Doctrine: telegraphed tells (no blind deaths),
always bypassable, live mostly in CHALLENGE sections, TEACH stays clean.

## Archetypes
| # | Archetype | Behavior class |
|---|-----------|-----------------|
| 1 | Covered pit | Fall = life loss; sag lip / texture misalignment is the tell |
| 2 | Roller | Spinning log/rock over a gap; timing + momentum |
| 3 | Telegraphed drop | Hazard shakes, then drops; position puzzle |
| 4 | Swarm | Nest spawns pursuit until player reaches water/safe zone |
| 5 | Forced slide | Slope momentum with obstacles; punishes panic |
| 6 | Stagger snare | Brief root/ice stumble; not lethal alone, deadly next to enemies |

## Placement rules (from Level Design Doctrine)
- CHALLENGE sections only on first appearance; may appear in DEVELOP as teaching pairs.
- Every trap teaches with a "free" first encounter (survivable or clearly telegraphed).
- Never two lethal traps back-to-back without a checkpoint between.
- Combos: trap + hound (trap 6), trap + drone (nest trigger) are the region's signature pairings.
